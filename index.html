FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Why Popcix Creative Studio instead of a full-time in-house team?

We provide senior-level creative direction, design, and development agility without the overhead, recruitment delays, or long-term liabilities of full-time staffing.

How fast is project delivery?

Most sprint tasks are delivered within 2-3 business days. We balance rapid turnaround with uncompromising craft and meticulous quality assurance.

What does the Popcix workflow process look like?

We follow a transparent, iterative process: Discovery & Strategy → Concept & Wireframing → High-Fidelity UI/UX & Motion → Production & Deployment.

How do we initiate and manage requests?

You can manage project requests directly via our dedicated client board, Slack channel, or weekly strategy calls with your assigned creative director.

What tools and tech stack do you work in?

We craft designs in Figma, After Effects, and Cinema 4D, and build high-performance web experiences using HTML5, modern JavaScript, GSAP, WebGL, and Next.js.
